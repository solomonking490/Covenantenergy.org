What this proof of concept shows

It shows

  • The six-step alert chain working end to end
  • Rules that reject a single-reading spike before anyone is paged
  • Escalation to a supervisor when nobody acknowledges
  • Backup power carrying the node during a grid outage
  • Offline buffering and replay with original timestamps
  • A fleet view split into active and maintenance sites

It does not show

  • Sensor accuracy on a real transformer
  • A measured false-alarm rate
  • How real responders behave at night
  • Battery life through a long outage

The 20-site pilot, six months and 120 transformer-months, tests each of these. A no-scale result is a valid result.

The case behind it: Idera Oduguyan, Ikorodu

Between February and August 2026 this community’s transformer was vandalized twice for its copper, and an undetected fault left another unit overheating until it had to be replaced. The community paid the bill itself. TransGuard senses oil level, temperature, load, fire and access, so a break-in or a developing overheat reaches a named responder while there is still something to save.
